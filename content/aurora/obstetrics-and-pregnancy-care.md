## About the service

Dr Aye Thidar Htun is Aurora's Obstetrician & Gynaecologist. She consults at Earlwood and provides obstetric care for women who prefer a single specialist through their pregnancy, and gynaecological consultations for women whose concerns need specialist review.

Obstetric care through pregnancy with a specialist obstetrician means you see the same clinician from confirmation through your antenatal visits, and Dr Htun is the doctor who is aware of your case at delivery. There's no hand-off at 36 weeks that patients often experience in shared care models. For women who value continuity through birth, or whose history or current pregnancy warrants specialist-led care from the start, this is the model.

Gynaecological consultations cover the conditions and concerns that sit outside routine general practice. Menstrual disturbance that needs investigation, pelvic pain, endometriosis assessment and management, fibroids, ovarian cysts, polycystic ovary syndrome that needs specialist input, prolapse, urinary incontinence, and pre-conception review after previous pregnancy complications.

Aurora GPs continue to provide primary women's health care alongside Dr Htun's specialist role. Many patients see their Aurora GP for routine care and are referred to Dr Htun for specific specialist input when their GP identifies a need.

## Pregnancy care with Dr Htun

Pregnancy care with an obstetrician typically starts as soon as pregnancy is confirmed. Your first appointment covers your history, screening options, and a plan for the pregnancy that suits your circumstances.

Subsequent visits follow the standard antenatal schedule, more frequently as you approach term. Between visits, Dr Htun coordinates with the hospital where you'll deliver, arranges ultrasounds, reviews blood tests, and manages any issues that arise during the pregnancy.

Delivery is at the hospital where Dr Htun has admitting rights, arranged as part of your care plan.

Postnatal care includes hospital review, six-week postnatal check, contraception discussion, and coordination back to your Aurora GP for ongoing family and women's health care.

## Gynaecological consultations

Referral to Dr Htun for gynaecological review is appropriate when your GP identifies a concern that benefits from specialist assessment. Common reasons include:

- Persistent or worsening pelvic pain
- Heavy or irregular menstrual bleeding not resolving with first-line management
- Suspected endometriosis
- Fibroids causing symptoms
- Ovarian cysts identified on imaging
- Polycystic ovary syndrome with fertility implications
- Menopausal symptoms not settling with first-line care
- Recurrent miscarriage
- Fertility concerns
- Prolapse or urinary symptoms

Some conditions can be managed with medical treatment; others require surgical review. Dr Htun performs relevant procedures at her admitting hospitals.

## Fees and billing

Specialist obstetric and gynaecological consultations are privately billed. A GP referral is required for the Medicare rebate to apply. Without a valid referral in place, the consultation is fully private.

Fees for obstetric care through pregnancy are structured differently from single consultation appointments. Reception confirms Dr Htun's fee structure and expected out-of-pocket cost when you book.

## FAQ

**Do I need a GP referral to see Dr Htun?**
Yes. A GP referral is required for the Medicare rebate to apply. Your usual GP or an Aurora GP can prepare the referral.

**Can Aurora GPs refer to Dr Htun?**
Yes. If you're already seeing an Aurora GP and they identify a concern that needs specialist review, they will refer internally.

**Which hospital would I deliver at with Dr Htun?**
Delivery arrangements are discussed at your first consultation and are based on Dr Htun's current admitting hospital arrangements and your preferences.

**Is Dr Htun a public or private obstetrician?**
Private. Fees apply.

**What if I've had a shared care pregnancy before and want obstetric care this time?**
That's a common reason to shift models. Dr Htun's initial consultation is the right place to talk through your previous pregnancy and plan this one.

**Can I see Dr Htun for gynaecological issues if I'm not pregnant?**
Yes. Gynaecological consultations are a substantial part of Dr Htun's practice.
