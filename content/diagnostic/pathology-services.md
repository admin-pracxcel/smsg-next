## About pathology at SMSG

Pathology is the range of laboratory tests your doctor uses to investigate, diagnose and monitor health conditions. Blood tests are the most common, but pathology also includes urine tests, microbiology cultures, cytology, histology (tissue examination), and other specimen analysis.

At SMSG, pathology collection is available on-site during opening hours. Your GP or specialist orders the test, you have your sample taken at reception or during your appointment, and the sample is processed by our pathology partner. Results are returned directly to the ordering doctor for review and follow-up.

For samples that need to be taken in a specific way or at a specific time (fasting bloods, morning cortisol, specific timed samples), instructions are provided at the time of ordering.

---

## What pathology covers

**Blood tests.** Full blood count, iron studies, thyroid function, kidney function, liver function, blood glucose, HbA1c, cholesterol and lipid profiles, hormone tests, vitamin levels, autoimmune markers, blood-borne virus screening, and many others.

**Urine tests.** Routine urinalysis, urine culture, urine albumin-creatinine ratio, drug screens where clinically or occupationally required.

**Microbiology cultures.** Throat swabs, wound swabs, and other cultures for suspected infection.

**Faecal tests.** Faecal occult blood tests (bowel cancer screening), stool cultures, faecal calprotectin, and other faecal analyses.

**Cytology and histology.** Cervical Screening Test samples (see [Cervical Screening](/cervical-screening/)) and biopsy specimens from procedures like skin excisions (see [Excision Procedures](/excision-procedures/)) are processed through our pathology partner.

**Cardiac and other biomarkers.** Where cardiac, inflammatory or specific disease markers are ordered.

## How to have pathology done

**Test ordered during a consultation.** If your GP or specialist orders pathology during your appointment, the sample can often be taken on the day. For tests that require specific preparation (fasting), you may return for the sample on another day.

**Test ordered without a consultation.** If you have a pathology request form from a previous consultation or from an external doctor, you can attend reception during opening hours to have the sample taken. Bring the request form with you.

**Fasting bloods.** Some tests require fasting (no food or drink other than water) for a set period beforehand. Your GP will let you know if fasting is needed, and typically these samples are taken first thing in the morning.

**Timed samples.** Some tests must be taken at specific times of day (early morning cortisol) or under specific conditions. Your GP will explain the timing.

## Fees and billing

Most pathology tests ordered under Medicare are bulk-billed to Medicare, meaning there's no out-of-pocket cost for the patient. This includes standard blood tests, urine tests, and most other routine investigations.

Some specialised or specific tests are not eligible for a Medicare rebate, and are privately billed. Your GP will let you know if this applies to a specific test being ordered.

Non-Medicare pathology (private tests, drug screens for employment or legal purposes, specific tests for insurance) is charged privately. Fees vary by test.

The consultation with your GP or specialist attracts its own fee separate from the pathology tests ordered.

---

## Getting your results

Results are returned to the ordering doctor, typically within 1 to 5 business days for standard tests. More specialised tests take longer.

For non-urgent results, book a follow-up appointment with the ordering doctor to discuss them. See our [Results Policy](/patient-information/results-policy/) for details on how results are managed at SMSG.

Reception can confirm whether a result has arrived and whether your doctor has reviewed it, but cannot interpret results or provide clinical advice about them.

## FAQ

**Do I need to make an appointment for pathology?**
Not always. If you have a request form, you can attend reception during opening hours. For fasting bloods or timed samples, timing matters, so check with reception if you're unsure.

**Do I need to fast?**
Only for specific tests. Your GP will let you know if fasting is required. Fasting typically means no food or drink other than water for 8 to 12 hours before the test.

**Can I have pathology done at any SMSG centre?**
Yes. If your request form was written by an external doctor, you can still have the sample taken at any SMSG centre.

**Are all blood tests bulk-billed?**
Most standard blood tests are bulk-billed to Medicare. Some specialised tests and non-Medicare tests attract a private fee. Your GP will let you know if this applies.

**How long do results take?**
Standard blood tests: 1 to 3 business days. Imaging and specialised tests: 1 to 5 business days. Some specialised pathology (histology, genetic testing): up to 2 weeks.

**Can I have my Cervical Screening Test done here?**
Yes. Cervical Screening Tests are done during a consultation with a GP. See our [Cervical Screening page](/cervical-screening/).

**What if I can't reach reception for a result?**
Book a follow-up appointment with your GP. Results are the doctor's to explain and act on. See our [Results Policy](/patient-information/results-policy/).
