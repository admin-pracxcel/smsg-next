## Emergency

If you're experiencing a medical emergency, **dial 000 immediately**. Do not wait for an SMSG appointment for anything life-threatening. See our [Emergency Information](/patient-information/emergency-information/) page for the full list of situations requiring emergency care and nearest emergency departments.

## General enquiries

For enquiries about appointments, prescriptions, referrals or results, contact the centre where you usually attend.

If you're not sure which centre applies to your enquiry, phone any of the three centre numbers and reception will direct you.

## Feedback and complaints

We take feedback seriously. If you have concerns about your care or experience at SMSG, or if you'd like to share positive feedback about a clinician or team member:

- Speak to reception at the centre
- Phone the centre
- Email the centre directly

For formal or serious concerns, ask reception at the centre to escalate to the practice manager. See our [Feedback & Complaints](/about/feedback-complaints/) page for the full process.
