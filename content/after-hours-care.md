## Hero paragraph

When SMSG is closed and your concern is not a life-threatening emergency, there are three main options: call an after-hours home doctor service, speak to a nurse through Healthdirect, or wait until we open. This page covers what to do outside our opening hours for non-emergency situations. For emergencies, see our [Emergency Information](/patient-information/emergency-information/) page.

## SMSG opening hours

**Earlwood Medical Centre.** Monday to Friday 9am to 6pm, Saturday 9am to 3pm, Sunday closed.

**Bangor Medical Centre.** Monday to Friday 9am to 6pm, Saturday and Sunday closed.

**Sans Souci Doctors.** Monday to Friday 9am to 6pm, Saturday and Sunday closed.

Outside these hours, the after-hours options below apply.

## National Home Doctor Service (13 SICK)

For non-emergency medical care outside our hours, call the National Home Doctor Service on **13 SICK (13 74 25)**.

The National Home Doctor Service arranges for a bulk-billed doctor to visit you at home in the evening, overnight, on weekends, or on public holidays. This is a genuine home visit, not a telephone consultation. You do not need to be an SMSG patient to use this service.

Home visits are for conditions that need medical attention but don't warrant an emergency department, such as:

- Sudden onset of illness like fever, sore throat, or ear infection
- Vomiting, diarrhoea or mild dehydration
- Rashes and minor skin infections
- Urinary symptoms
- Minor injuries that don't require hospital care
- Follow-up on symptoms that have worsened after we've closed

The visiting doctor sends a report to your usual GP the next business day, so continuity of care is preserved.

Call **13 SICK (13 74 25)** or visit **homedoctor.com.au** to arrange a visit.

## Healthdirect (1800 022 222)

For non-urgent health advice, call **Healthdirect on 1800 022 222**. This is a free, 24-hour phone service staffed by registered nurses. Available every day of the year.

Healthdirect nurses can help you decide whether you need to see a doctor now, whether your symptoms can wait until we open, or whether you should attend an emergency department.

Healthdirect is not an emergency service. For emergencies, dial 000.

---

## When to use which service

**If you're not sure whether your symptoms need medical care,** call Healthdirect. A registered nurse will help you triage.

**If you know you need a doctor tonight but it's not an emergency,** call 13 SICK for a home visit.

**If it's life-threatening,** dial 000 or attend the nearest emergency department. See our [Emergency Information](/patient-information/emergency-information/) page.

---

## After your after-hours contact

If you've been seen by the National Home Doctor Service or spoken to Healthdirect, book a follow-up with your usual GP at SMSG on the next business day. Continuity of care matters, and your usual GP has the full context of your history.

The visiting after-hours doctor sends a report to SMSG. If you were seen by Healthdirect only, mention it to your GP at your next appointment so it can be added to your file.
