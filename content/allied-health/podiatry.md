## About podiatry at SMSG

Podiatrists work with the foot, ankle and lower limb. They assess how your feet function, treat conditions that affect the feet and lower limbs, and provide care for populations where foot health matters more than average, such as people with diabetes.

**Hana Rizk.** Podiatrist based at Earlwood. Consults through Kids' Dr for paediatric patients, and works with adults across a range of foot concerns. English, Arabic.

---

## What podiatry covers

**General foot and nail care.** Persistent nail problems, ingrown toenails, thickened nails, corns, callouses, warts and other common foot concerns that benefit from professional treatment.

**Biomechanical assessment.** Analysis of how your foot moves during walking and running. Assessment can identify contributors to foot pain, ankle pain, knee pain and lower back pain that come from foot function.

**Orthotics and footwear.** Where biomechanical issues contribute to pain or dysfunction, custom or off-the-shelf orthotics and footwear advice can help.

**Diabetes foot care.** For patients with diabetes, regular podiatry assessment is an important part of preventing complications. Diabetes can affect circulation and nerve sensation in the feet, and small problems can develop into more serious ones if not addressed.

**Paediatric podiatry (through Kids' Dr).** Assessment and treatment for children with foot pain, gait concerns, in-toeing or out-toeing, flat feet with symptoms, and other foot and lower limb concerns.

**Sports and overuse injuries.** Plantar fasciitis, Achilles tendinopathy, shin pain, stress-related foot pain, and other overuse injuries in active people.

**Post-injury care.** Rehabilitation after ankle sprains, fractures, and lower limb surgeries.

**Skin and nail conditions.** Fungal infections, plantar warts, and other skin conditions affecting the feet.

## What happens in an appointment

The first appointment involves:

- A history of your concern, including any relevant medical history
- A physical assessment of the feet and lower limbs
- Biomechanical assessment where relevant
- Discussion of findings and treatment options
- Any immediate treatment that's appropriate (nail care, callus removal, dressings)
- A plan for follow-up sessions where needed

Some concerns are resolved in a single appointment. Others benefit from a course of sessions, orthotics, or coordinated care with other clinicians.

## Fees and billing

Podiatry sessions are privately billed. Medicare rebates may apply under a Chronic Disease Management Plan prepared by your GP, which allows access to a limited number of rebated sessions per year.

For patients with diabetes, an annual foot check is generally included in diabetes care and may be arranged as part of your Chronic Disease Management Plan.

Private health fund rebates apply for patients with podiatry extras cover; check with your health fund.

For NDIS participants, podiatry may be funded through your NDIS plan. Discuss at booking.

Reception confirms specific fee arrangements at booking.

## FAQ

**Do I need a referral?**
No. Podiatry can be booked directly. A GP referral under a Chronic Disease Management Plan is required to access the Medicare rebate.

**How long is a session?**
Initial appointments are typically 30 to 60 minutes depending on the assessment needed. Follow-up appointments are usually 20 to 45 minutes.

**Do I need to have diabetes to have a foot check?**
No. Foot checks are useful for people with diabetes because of the specific risks, but anyone with foot concerns can book.

**Can podiatrists cut toenails?**
Yes. Podiatrists provide nail care as part of routine podiatry, particularly for patients whose nails are difficult to manage due to thickness, shape, arthritis or vision.

**What about custom orthotics?**
Custom orthotics can be prescribed where the biomechanical assessment shows they'll help. Cost of custom orthotics is additional to the consultation and depends on the specific type.

**Can Hana see children?**
Yes. Paediatric podiatry is available through Kids' Dr with Hana at Earlwood.

**What about corns and callouses?**
Yes. Corn and callus removal is straightforward podiatry care that provides immediate relief.
