## About physiotherapy at SMSG

Physiotherapy addresses movement and function. Physiotherapists assess how you move, identify what's contributing to pain or restriction, and work with you on treatment that reduces symptoms and improves function.

Two physiotherapists consult at SMSG:

- **Antonio Kim.** Physiotherapist based at Earlwood. English, Korean.
- **Daniel Tran.** Physiotherapist across Earlwood and Sans Souci, with a paediatric focus through Kids' Dr. English, Vietnamese.

Physiotherapy sits within general adult care and, for children, within our Kids' Dr multidisciplinary team.

---

## What physiotherapy covers

**Musculoskeletal assessment and treatment.** Neck and back pain, joint pain, sports injuries, repetitive strain, postural concerns, and pain that hasn't settled with time or self-management.

**Injury rehabilitation.** Recovery from acute injuries, post-surgical rehabilitation, and return-to-activity programs.

**Chronic pain support.** Physiotherapy is one part of managing chronic pain, often alongside medical review, psychology, and lifestyle change.

**Paediatric physiotherapy (Daniel Tran).** Developmental physiotherapy for infants and children, gross motor concerns, coordination difficulties, and post-injury rehabilitation in children. Coordinated with Kids' Dr paediatricians.

**Post-hospital rehabilitation.** Recovery after hospital admission, particularly after orthopaedic procedures, cardiac events, or extended illness where deconditioning is a factor.

**Fall prevention.** Balance assessment and exercises for adults at risk of falls.

## What happens in an appointment

The first appointment involves:

- A detailed history of your concern
- A physical assessment relevant to what's happening
- Discussion of the likely contributors and treatment options
- Hands-on treatment where appropriate
- An exercise program to practise between sessions
- A plan for follow-up

Physiotherapy is generally not a single-session fix. Most conditions benefit from a course of appointments spaced over weeks to months. Your physiotherapist will discuss the expected timeframe at your first visit.

For paediatric appointments with Daniel Tran, sessions are structured around the child's age and situation. Family members are welcome to participate.

## Fees and billing

Physiotherapy sessions are privately billed. Medicare rebates may apply under a Chronic Disease Management Plan prepared by your GP; this typically allows access to a limited number of bulk-billed or partially rebated sessions per year.

Private health fund rebates apply for patients with physiotherapy extras cover; check with your health fund.

Reception confirms specific fee arrangements at booking.

## FAQ

**Do I need a referral for physiotherapy?**
No. Physiotherapy can be booked directly. A GP referral under a Chronic Disease Management Plan is required if you want to access the Medicare rebate for eligible sessions.

**How long is a physiotherapy appointment?**
Initial appointments are typically 30 to 45 minutes. Follow-up appointments are usually 20 to 30 minutes.

**How many sessions will I need?**
Depends on the condition. Your physiotherapist will discuss expected timeframes at the first appointment.

**Can I claim on my private health fund?**
Yes, if you have physiotherapy extras cover. Ask reception to process the claim on the day using your health fund card.

**Is Daniel Tran a paediatric physiotherapist only?**
Daniel has a paediatric focus through Kids' Dr, and also sees adults. Reception can confirm what he's currently taking bookings for.

**Do you do WorkCover or CTP physiotherapy?**
These claims are typically handled through our external partner Synergy Medical. Mention the claim type at booking so reception can direct you appropriately.
