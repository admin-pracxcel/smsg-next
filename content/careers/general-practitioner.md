## About the role

SMSG Consultant Specialist GPs consult as independent practitioners, with the group providing the premises, patient administration, nursing support and coordination across disciplines. The consulting model gives clinical autonomy while removing the administrative overhead of running an independent practice on your own.

You'd be part of a group that includes:

- 33 Consultant Specialist GPs across the three centres
- 17 Excelsia Specialist Centre consultants
- 12 allied health practitioners
- Nursing team including senior RNs
- Practice management and administration

Patient continuity is strong. Many of our patients have been with the group for years or across generations, and internal referrals within SMSG and its sub-brands (Aurora, Kids' Dr, Excelsia, Clarion, Sydney Cosmedic) create rich clinical variety.

---

## What we're looking for

**Essential**

- Current AHPRA registration as a General Practitioner
- Fellowship (FRACGP) or equivalent
- Vocationally registered (VR)
- Alignment with patient-centred, evidence-based general practice

**Welcomed**

- Special interest in skin cancer medicine (Clarion Skin Cancer Clinic)
- Special interest in women's health, obstetrics or maternal care (Aurora Women & Babies Health)
- Special interest in paediatrics (Kids' Dr at Sans Souci)
- Procedural skills including minor surgery
- Chronic disease management and complex patient care
- Language skills relevant to the community (Cantonese, Mandarin, Vietnamese, Arabic, Greek, Italian and others are all valued)

---

## What SMSG offers

**Multi-disciplinary support.** On-site pathology collection, allied health team across dietetics, physiotherapy, psychology, speech pathology and podiatry, plus consultant specialists in ten specialties through Excelsia.

**Modern facilities.** Purpose-set-up centres with dedicated treatment rooms, skin procedure rooms at all three centres, and modern equipment.

**Nursing and administration support.** Experienced nursing team and Patient Support Officers handle patient flow, so your consulting time is focused on clinical work.

**Best Practice clinical software.** Standardised across the group, with training provided.

**Recognition.** Great Place to Work® certified. 2023 CESPHN Certificate of Excellence in General Practice. AGPAL-accredited.

**Clinical variety.** Patient mix across the three centres includes complex chronic disease, families, women's and maternal care, paediatrics, procedural work and skin cancer medicine.

**Career development.** Support for sub-specialty interests, opportunities to consult across sub-brands, and pathways into leadership within the group.

---

## Special interests we're keen to support

**Skin cancer medicine.** Our Clarion Skin Cancer Clinic team includes GPs with procedural interest across all three centres. Dedicated skin procedure rooms and experienced nursing support for excisions.

**Women's and maternal health.** Aurora Women & Babies Health includes antenatal shared care, obstetric care through Dr Aye Thidar Htun, and comprehensive women's health across the life course.

**Paediatric care.** Kids' Dr, based at Sans Souci with extended presence at Earlwood, supports GPs with a paediatric interest through our multidisciplinary team.

**Chronic disease.** Endocrinology, Nephrology, Cardiology and General Medicine consultants at Excelsia support GP-led chronic disease management.

**Cantonese-speaking GPs.** Not essential, but warmly welcomed by our diverse community patient base.

## Working arrangements

Full-time and part-time consulting positions available across Earlwood, Bangor and Sans Souci. Individual consulting patterns are agreed between you and the group.

Saturday consulting is available at Earlwood if you'd like to include weekend hours in your pattern.

Fee structure and administrative arrangements are discussed at interview.
