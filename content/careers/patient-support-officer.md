## About the role

Patient Support Officers are the first point of contact for patients and other health professionals across all three SMSG centres. Day-to-day work includes:

- Appointment scheduling for GPs, specialists and allied health
- Patient enquiries by phone, email and in person
- Billing, receipting and Medicare processing
- Document scanning and data entry
- Investigation coordination and results follow-up
- General administrative support to the clinical team
- Coordination with allied health and external providers

You'll work in a large, dynamic team, and the position may involve consulting across our three centres depending on business needs.

---

## What we're looking for

**Essential**

- High level of maturity, as the first point of contact for patients and professionals
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills
- Organisational skills including time management, accuracy, attention to detail
- Ability to multi-task and work efficiently under pressure
- Team player, comfortable in a large dynamic team
- Adaptability across changing situations

**Preferred**

- Experience with Best Practice Medical Software
- Experience with HICAPs terminals
- Previous experience in medical, allied health or clinical administration
- Flexibility with hours (4 to 5 days per week, with or without weekends)
- University graduates
- Flexibility to work across our three centres

**Language skills.** Multilingual applicants are warmly welcomed. Languages of particular value to our community include Greek, Cantonese, Mandarin, Italian, Arabic and Mongolian.

---

## Career pathway

This role is designed for people who want to build a long-term career in healthcare, not just do reception work. It offers a clear pathway into practice management and specialised areas including accounts, finance, HR, quality, projects and business development.

**Management & Leadership Development Program.** SMSG runs a dedicated development program that supports Patient Support Officers to move into higher-level roles over time. The program includes:

- 1:1 development check-ins
- Targeted workshops
- Job shadowing across sites and teams
- Cross-training across different areas of the business

**"Try on" different areas.** Over time, you'll be encouraged to explore tasks in HR, finance, operations and patient experience, and work with senior staff who are invested in mentoring you.

**Career progression.** Many of our senior administrators and Practice Operations team members started in Patient Support Officer roles and progressed internally as they demonstrated initiative, reliability and capability.

Higher-level tasks that can develop over time include reporting, rostering support, billing and accounts, audit and quality activities, recruitment assistance, or supporting new projects and service launches.

## What SMSG offers

**Growth-focused culture.** SMSG has a genuine commitment to developing people internally. If you're prepared to invest in your growth, we support the journey.

**Cross-site experience.** With three centres in the group, you get exposure to different centre cultures, patient bases and administrative rhythms.

**Modern systems.** Best Practice Medical Software and HICAPs across all three centres.

**A large, supportive team.** The Patient Support Officer team includes fifteen PSOs plus four Supervisors across the group.

**Recognition.** Great Place to Work® certified. 2023 CESPHN Certificate of Excellence in General Practice. AGPAL-accredited.

**Working arrangements.** Casual, part-time and full-time available. Rostering discussed to suit your circumstances.
