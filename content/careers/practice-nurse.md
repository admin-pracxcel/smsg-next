## About the role

Practice Nurses at SMSG work across the range of general practice nursing: treatment room procedures, clinical support to GPs, immunisations, chronic disease coordination, iron infusions, wound care, ECGs, spirometry and patient triage. Days are varied and no two are quite the same.

The nursing team is central to how the group works. We coordinate patient flow, support GPs with procedures, and are often the person a patient sees before, during and after a consultation. It's clinical work with genuine breadth.

You'd be part of a team that includes seven internal nursing team members across the three centres, with senior RNs leading the clinical direction of the team.

---

## What we're looking for

**Essential**

- Current AHPRA registration as a Registered Nurse (RN) or Enrolled Nurse (EEN)
- Willingness to work across the range of general practice nursing
- Team player who can work autonomously when needed
- Interest in ongoing professional development

**Welcomed**

- Experience with Best Practice clinical software (training provided)
- Previous general practice experience
- Skills in specific areas (immunisations, wound care, chronic disease, paediatric nursing, women's health)
- Language skills relevant to the community

**New graduates.** Welcome to apply. New graduates are mentored by our senior RNs and can develop their skills across the full range of practice nursing.

---

## What SMSG offers

**Mentorship.** All newly recruited nurses are mentored by senior RNs in the team. Development is practical and hands-on across a range of clinical work.

**Variety.** Practice nursing at SMSG covers immunisations, treatment room procedures (iron infusions, wound care, ECG, spirometry, ear syringing, cryotherapy), chronic disease coordination, procedural assistance, and paediatric nursing where relevant.

**Multi-site opportunity.** With three centres in the group, there's flexibility to work across sites or focus at one, depending on your preference and centre needs.

**Best Practice clinical software.** Standard across the group with training provided.

**Career progression.** Opportunity to take on a leadership role within the nursing team as your skills develop.

**Recognition.** Great Place to Work® certified. 2023 CESPHN Certificate of Excellence in General Practice. AGPAL-accredited.

**Working arrangements.** Casual, part-time and full-time all available. Rostering is discussed to suit your circumstances.

---

## What the work looks like day-to-day

**Treatment room procedures.** ECGs, spirometry, iron infusions, wound care and dressings, ear syringing, cryotherapy, injections.

**Immunisation.** Adult and childhood immunisations under the National Immunisation Program, plus travel and other private vaccinations.

**Chronic disease support.** Coordinating GP Management Plans, follow-up on care plan actions, patient education.

**Procedural assistance.** Supporting GPs and specialists during skin procedures, minor surgery, and other in-centre procedures.

**Patient triage.** Assessing patients on presentation and coordinating with the treating doctor.

**Care coordination.** Working with allied health, specialists and hospitals on patient care pathways.

## Working arrangements

Casual, part-time and full-time roles available at Earlwood, Bangor and Sans Souci. Some roles involve rotation across sites; others are based primarily at one centre.

Saturday hours are available at Earlwood if you'd like to include weekend work in your pattern.

Rostering and pay arrangements are discussed at interview.
