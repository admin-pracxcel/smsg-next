Ongoing interest in allied health practitioners across our three centres. Speech pathology, occupational therapy, physiotherapy, dietetics, psychology, counselling and podiatry. Both employed and consulting arrangements considered, depending on the discipline.

## About the opportunity

Allied health at SMSG works closely with our GPs, consultant specialists and nursing team. Coordination between disciplines is central to how the group works, and allied health practitioners are integral to that.

Our current allied health team covers:

- **Physiotherapy** with Antonio Kim and Daniel Tran
- **Psychology** with Sandra Bell, Sue Boursiani, Cara Chillari and Nita Hidalgo
- **Counselling and psychotherapy** with Julia Magrin and Thao Tammy Trang
- **Speech pathology** with Sarah Impellizzeri (plus TLC visiting service)
- **Dietetics** with Dr Xue-Fei Fay Yu PhD and Wing Tung Stephanie Yu
- **Podiatry** with Hana Rizk

We're open to expanding across each of these disciplines, and to conversations with practitioners in occupational therapy and other allied health areas we don't currently have on the team.

## Disciplines we hire across

**Physiotherapy.** Musculoskeletal, sports, paediatric, and general practice-adjacent physiotherapy. Adult and paediatric work available through the group and Kids' Dr.

**Psychology.** Clinical, counselling, and general psychology across adult, adolescent and paediatric. Perinatal and women's mental health work through Aurora. Paediatric and family work through Kids' Dr.

**Counselling and psychotherapy.** Adult and family counselling across a range of modalities. Trauma-informed practice for Victim Services counselling.

**Speech pathology.** Paediatric focus through Kids' Dr with scope for adult work depending on interest and demand.

**Dietetics.** Chronic disease, paediatric nutrition, women's health, weight management and gastrointestinal work.

**Podiatry.** General podiatry across adult and paediatric, diabetes foot care, and biomechanical assessment.

**Occupational therapy.** Not currently on the internal team, but of interest for the right practitioner. Paediatric OT would complement Kids' Dr; adult OT could work alongside our physiotherapy and rehabilitation focus.

---

## What we're looking for

**Essential across all disciplines**

- Current AHPRA registration where relevant, or membership of the appropriate professional body (Speech Pathology Australia, DAA for dietetics, PACFA or ACA for counselling)
- Alignment with evidence-based, patient-centred allied health practice
- Interest in working within a multi-disciplinary team

**Welcomed**

- Special interests in areas that complement our existing team
- Experience with Best Practice clinical software or willingness to learn
- Language skills relevant to the community
- Interest in taking on paediatric work through Kids' Dr, women's health through Aurora, or specific chronic disease areas

---

## What SMSG offers

**Coordinated multi-disciplinary practice.** Direct working relationships with our GPs, consultant specialists and nursing team. Internal referrals within the group flow easily, and clinical coordination is genuine.

**Established patient base.** Our allied health practitioners work with patients referred internally and externally, drawing on a large group patient base.

**Modern facilities.** Purpose-set-up centres with modern equipment and dedicated treatment spaces.

**Flexible arrangements.** Both employed and room-rental arrangements considered depending on the discipline and the situation.

**Cross-site opportunity.** Practitioners can consult at one centre or across the group depending on need and preference.

**Recognition.** Great Place to Work® certified. 2023 CESPHN Certificate of Excellence in General Practice. AGPAL-accredited.

**Kids' Dr and Aurora.** For practitioners with a paediatric or women's health focus, our sub-brand structure provides a defined patient pathway and multi-disciplinary support.

## How to apply

**Send your resume and a brief note to recruitment@smsg.au.**

In your note, please include:

- The allied health discipline you practise
- Your qualifications and current registration or professional membership
- Whether you're looking for an employed role, a room-rental arrangement, or open to either
- Preferred centre or centres, or open to any
- Days and hours you'd want to work
- Any special interests or additional information you'd like us to know

Our recruitment team reviews all allied health enquiries. Depending on the discipline and current group needs, we may respond quickly, or we may keep your details on file for when the right opportunity emerges. Either way, we'll be in touch.
