## Hero paragraph

Specialist Medical Services Group is a three-centre practice group in Sydney's Inner West and south, home to 60+ clinicians and a large support team. We hire for clinical, administrative and allied health roles across Earlwood, Bangor and Sans Souci. If you're considering a move, this is where the current roles sit and how to get in touch.

---

## The group at a glance

Three centres. Earlwood Medical Centre (Homer Street), Bangor Medical Centre (Yala Road), Sans Souci Doctors (Campbell Street). Each with its own team, patient base and clinical focus, all working within the same group.

Sixty-plus clinicians. Consultant Specialist GPs, Excelsia Specialist Centre consultants across ten specialties, Aurora Women & Babies Health clinicians, Kids' Dr paediatricians and allied health, Clarion Skin Cancer Clinic team, and Sydney Cosmedic.

Great Place to Work® certified. Award-winning practices, including the 2023 CESPHN Certificate of Excellence in General Practice.

AGPAL-accredited across all three centres.

---

## Why SMSG

**Multidisciplinary by design.** Our team includes GPs, consultant specialists, allied health and nursing, all working from the same premises. Coordination between disciplines is close and consultative.

**Modern facilities.** Purpose-set-up centres with dedicated treatment rooms, on-site pathology collection, and skin procedure rooms.

**Consultative culture.** Independent practitioners work alongside a support team that's built around clinical care. Practice managers, senior RNs, and a leadership team who know your name.

**Learning and development.** Support for GP registrars, mentorship for nurses, and structured development for administration and management pathways.

**Language diversity.** Our team collectively speaks more than a dozen languages, reflecting the communities we serve. Multilingual applicants are welcomed across every role.

**Location.** Two centres in southern Sydney (Bangor, Sans Souci) and one in the Inner West (Earlwood), all within reach of the wider Sydney area and public transport.

## Current roles

We currently recruit across four ongoing role streams. Click through for the specifics of each role, what we're looking for, and how to apply.

### [General Practitioner](/careers/general-practitioner/)

FRACGP or VR GPs for full-time or part-time consulting positions across all three centres. Special interests in skin cancer medicine, women's health, procedural work and chronic disease welcomed.

### [Practice Nurse](/careers/practice-nurse/)

EEN or RN roles across the three centres. Casual, part-time and full-time. New graduates welcome; mentored by senior RNs in the team.

### [Patient Support Officer](/careers/patient-support-officer/)

Patient-facing administration and coordination roles across the three centres. Career pathway into practice management, HR, quality, and business development through our Management & Leadership Development Program.

### [Allied Health](/careers/allied-health/)

Ongoing interest in speech pathology, occupational therapy, physiotherapy, dietetics, psychology, counselling and podiatry roles. Both employed and room-rental arrangements considered depending on the discipline.

## How to apply

**Register interest through the relevant role page.** Each role page has an interest form for submitting your details and resume.

**Or send your resume to recruitment@smsg.au.** Attach a brief note about the role that interests you, your current situation and when you'd be available to start.

Our recruitment team reviews all applications and will be in touch about next steps. Confidentiality is respected throughout the process.
