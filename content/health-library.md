## Post listing

Simple grid or vertical list of published articles. No categories, no filters, no search.

Each post card shows: featured image, article title, one-sentence excerpt, author name and role (e.g. "Dr Lisa Cheng, Consultant Specialist GP"), publish date, and a link through to the article page.

Sort by publish date, most recent first. Paginate at 12 posts per page.

## Build notes

- Two-section layout: hero (title, one-sentence intro) then post listing.
- Article template for individual posts is a separate spec, not covered here. Individual articles will live at `/health-library/[post-slug]/`.
- Empty state at launch: if no articles are published yet, show a short line where the listing would sit, for example, "New articles publishing shortly. In the meantime, our [services](/) and [team](/team/) pages are the best starting points."
- No sidebar, no featured post callout, no "popular articles" section for the first version. Keep it simple.
- Author name links through to the author's individual profile page once those are built. Until then, author name is text-only.
