## What medicals and assessments cover

### Pre-employment medicals (Max-Health)

Pre-employment medicals confirm you meet the health requirements for a new role. These are typically requested by an employer as part of the hiring process, particularly for roles involving physical demands, safety-critical tasks, or specific health requirements (transport, mining, construction, emergency services, healthcare).

SMSG provides pre-employment medicals under our Max-Health service. Depending on the role and the employer's requirements, the medical may include:

- Medical history review
- Physical examination
- Vision assessment
- Hearing assessment (audiometry)
- Spirometry (lung function)
- ECG
- Blood tests
- Urine tests including drug and alcohol screening
- Musculoskeletal assessment
- Specific fitness for role assessment

Bring any forms provided by your employer or the assessing body, plus your identification. If the employer has specific requirements not covered by the standard medical, mention this at booking so the appointment can be structured accordingly.

### Driver's licence and commercial medicals

Driver's licence medicals are required for certain classes of licence in NSW and for older drivers. Common types include:

- **Standard driver's licence medical.** Required by Transport for NSW for drivers over 75 and for drivers with certain health conditions.
- **Commercial vehicle driver's licence medical.** Required for heavy vehicle, bus, taxi, ride-share and other commercial licences. Follows the National Assessing Fitness to Drive standard.
- **Learner driver medical.** Required in some circumstances (medical conditions declared during application).

Medical assessment includes vision, hearing, cardiovascular assessment, examination for conditions that could affect driving safety, and completion of the Transport for NSW medical form. Some conditions may require specialist input alongside the GP assessment.

Bring the assessment form from Transport for NSW, your current licence, glasses if you wear them, and a list of your medications.

### Veterans' Service (VES)

SMSG Earlwood provides Veterans' Service (VES) medical reviews for eligible individuals under the VES program. Veterans' Service (VES) is a US-based network that arranges medical reviews for American veterans and other eligible individuals located in Australia, coordinating with the assessing body.

For patients coordinating with the VES program, contact reception at Earlwood on 02 9554 7788 to discuss whether SMSG can assist with your specific requirements.

More information on the VES program is available at ves.com.

## Fees and billing

Medical assessments are typically privately billed. The fee reflects the length and complexity of the assessment, which varies significantly depending on what's required. Simple driver's medicals are shorter and less expensive than comprehensive pre-employment medicals with multiple components.

Medical assessments for employment, licences and legal purposes are generally not eligible for a Medicare rebate, as they're not a Medicare-funded service.

Reception confirms specific fees at booking based on the type of assessment.

## FAQ

**Do I need a referral for a medical assessment?**
No. Medical assessments are booked directly.

**How long does a pre-employment medical take?**
Depending on what the employer requires, anywhere from 30 minutes to two hours. Simple medicals with just history and examination are shorter. Assessments including audiometry, spirometry, ECG, blood tests and drug and alcohol screening take longer. Reception will estimate at booking.

**What should I bring?**
Your identification, any forms provided by the employer or assessing body, glasses if you wear them, a list of current medications, and hearing aids if you use them.

**Can the results be sent directly to my employer?**
With your consent, yes. Your GP or reception can send results directly to the assessing party.

**How long does a driver's licence medical take?**
Typically 15 to 30 minutes for a standard medical. Longer if you have specific conditions requiring more detailed assessment.

**What happens if I don't pass the medical?**
The GP discusses the reasons with you. For some conditions, treatment or specialist input can restore fitness. For others, restrictions may apply to your licence or role. Your GP will explain your options.

**Can I do a medical without an appointment?**
No. Medical assessments require a dedicated booked appointment.
