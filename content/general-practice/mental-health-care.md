## How mental health care works at SMSG

Mental health concerns don't fit a single pathway. Some patients need one or two conversations with their GP and a plan to try things at home. Others benefit from a Mental Health Care Plan and structured therapy. Some need specialist psychiatric input alongside GP-led care. Your GP works with you to find the right approach.

### Mental Health Care Plans

A Mental Health Care Plan is a Medicare-funded structured plan prepared by your GP. It documents your mental health concern, the treatment plan, and the referral pathway to a psychologist, counsellor, social worker, or occupational therapist for structured therapy.

Under a Mental Health Care Plan, you can access up to ten Medicare-rebated psychology or allied mental health sessions per calendar year. The first six sessions are recommended and reviewed by your GP, with the option of a further four sessions with GP review.

Conditions commonly managed with a Mental Health Care Plan include:

- Anxiety disorders
- Depression
- Post-traumatic stress
- Adjustment reactions and life stressors
- Grief and bereavement
- Sleep concerns tied to mental health
- Chronic pain with mental health impact
- Eating concerns
- Substance use concerns

The Mental Health Care Plan is prepared during a longer GP consultation. Your GP will confirm the plan is right for your situation and coordinate the referral.

### Counselling and psychology at SMSG

SMSG has a team of psychologists, counsellors and a clinical psychologist who work with adults, adolescents and children across a range of presentations. Referral is through your GP under a Mental Health Care Plan or you can book directly.

Psychology and counselling at SMSG spans:

**Adult psychology.** Anxiety, depression, adjustment concerns, grief, trauma, relationship concerns, life transitions.

**Perinatal and women's mental health.** Coordinated with Aurora Women & Babies Health where relevant.

**Paediatric psychology.** Sandra Bell, Sue Boursiani and Cara Chillari work with children and adolescents through Kids' Dr.

**Counselling and psychotherapy.** Julia Magrin (Bangor and Sans Souci) and Thao Tammy Trang (Earlwood) offer counselling and psychotherapy for adults, adolescents and families.

**Clinical psychology.** Nita Hidalgo (Sans Souci and Earlwood) provides clinical psychology within Aurora.

### GP-led ongoing care

Not every mental health concern needs a formal plan or a referral. Sometimes the right approach is regular check-in appointments with your GP, medication where appropriate, and support while you work through what's happening.

Your GP can:

- Prescribe and monitor mental health medications
- Provide counselling within GP consultations
- Coordinate care with specialists where needed
- Support you through a difficult period without formal therapy
- Refer to specialist psychiatric care where the situation calls for it

## Fees and billing

Standard GP consultations for mental health concerns are billed under standard consultation rules. Mental Health Care Plan preparation attracts a specific Medicare item and is a longer consultation. Care plan reviews are shorter appointments with their own Medicare item.

Psychology and counselling sessions under a Mental Health Care Plan attract a Medicare rebate. The gap between the session fee and the rebate varies by practitioner. Reception can confirm the specific gap for a psychologist or counsellor you're being referred to.

Where a Mental Health Care Plan isn't in place, psychology or counselling sessions are privately billed without a Medicare rebate.

## FAQ

**Do I need a Mental Health Care Plan to see a psychologist?**
No, but it makes the sessions Medicare-rebatable. You can see a psychologist privately without a plan, and this may suit some patients (particularly where confidentiality preferences make a formal plan uncomfortable).

**How long is the Mental Health Care Plan consultation?**
It's a longer appointment than a standard consultation, typically 30 to 60 minutes.

**Can I see any psychologist under my Mental Health Care Plan?**
The plan can refer to any registered psychologist or allied mental health practitioner who bulk-bills or privately bills with a Medicare rebate. Your GP typically refers within SMSG for continuity of care, but you can request a specific practitioner.

**What if I need urgent mental health support?**
For a mental health crisis, dial 000 or attend an emergency department. For urgent but not life-threatening support, Lifeline (13 11 14) is available 24/7. See our [Emergency Information page](/patient-information/emergency-information/) for the full list of crisis support numbers.

**What about children and adolescents?**
Children and adolescents can access mental health care through your GP and Kids' Dr. Sandra Bell, Sue Boursiani and Cara Chillari work with young people.

**Does SMSG have a psychiatrist?**
SMSG does not currently have a psychiatrist on-site. Where psychiatric review is needed, your GP arranges a referral to an appropriate external specialist.
