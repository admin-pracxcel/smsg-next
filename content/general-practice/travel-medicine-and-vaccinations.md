## What travel medicine and vaccinations cover

### Pre-travel consultations

Book a travel consultation ideally 6 to 8 weeks before you leave. This gives time for vaccines to take effect (some require multiple doses spaced weeks apart) and for any specific arrangements to be made.

Your travel consultation covers:

- Destination-specific health risks
- Required and recommended vaccines
- Malaria prevention and other prophylactic medications
- Food and water safety
- Insect avoidance
- Altitude, climate and activity considerations
- Traveller's diarrhoea and its management
- Medications to take with you
- Travel medical kit essentials
- What to do if you get sick overseas
- Post-travel review if needed

For complex trips (long duration, remote destinations, multi-country itineraries) a longer consultation may be needed.

### Travel vaccinations

Travel vaccinations available at SMSG include the routine and commonly required travel vaccines. Common travel vaccines include:

- Hepatitis A and B
- Typhoid
- Tetanus, diphtheria and pertussis (if boosters are due)
- Influenza (seasonal)
- COVID-19 (as recommended)
- Meningococcal
- Japanese encephalitis
- Rabies
- Cholera

**Yellow Fever.** Yellow Fever vaccination is required for entry to certain countries. Yellow Fever can only be administered at centres approved by the Department of Health and Aged Care. If you need Yellow Fever vaccination, contact reception in advance to confirm current availability at your chosen centre. If Yellow Fever is not available at your preferred centre, we can direct you to an accredited centre nearby.

### Childhood vaccinations

Childhood immunisations under the National Immunisation Program (NIP) are free for children and are provided by any SMSG GP. The NIP schedule includes vaccines at birth, 6 weeks, 4 months, 6 months, 12 months, 18 months, 4 years, and adolescent years. Your child's individual schedule may vary based on medical history.

Immunisations are recorded in the Australian Immunisation Register (AIR) and can be accessed through your Medicare online account or the Express Plus Medicare app.

For families whose children are behind on the schedule (for any reason including moving from overseas), catch-up vaccinations can be planned during a longer appointment.

### Adult vaccinations

Adult vaccinations recommended under the National Immunisation Program include:

- Annual influenza
- COVID-19 boosters as recommended
- Pertussis (whooping cough) booster, particularly for pregnant women and grandparents
- Shingles vaccine for eligible adults
- Pneumococcal vaccine for eligible adults
- Tetanus and diphtheria boosters every 10 years

Ask your GP whether you're up to date on your recommended adult immunisations.

## Fees and billing

Standard consultations for travel medicine and vaccinations are billed under standard consultation rules. Longer consultations may attract a longer consultation fee with a Medicare rebate applying.

Vaccines under the National Immunisation Program are free. Travel vaccines are typically privately purchased; the cost varies by vaccine. Reception can provide an estimate at booking.

For adult vaccines funded under the NIP for eligible groups (such as annual influenza for over 65s, shingles for eligible adults), the vaccine itself is free; a consultation fee may apply for the appointment.

## FAQ

**When should I book my travel consultation?**
Ideally 6 to 8 weeks before you leave. Some vaccine schedules require multiple doses over several weeks. If you're travelling sooner than that, book anyway; we can prioritise the most important vaccines.

**Do I need a referral?**
No. Travel medicine and vaccinations are GP services.

**How do I find out what vaccines I need for my destination?**
Bring your itinerary to the consultation, including all countries you'll visit and stopover points. Your GP references current recommendations from Smartraveller and the Department of Health.

**Can I get Yellow Fever vaccination at SMSG?**
Yellow Fever administration requires specific accreditation. Contact reception to confirm current availability at your preferred centre. If not available, we can direct you to an accredited centre nearby.

**Are childhood vaccinations under the National Immunisation Program free?**
Yes. The vaccines themselves are free. A consultation fee may apply for the appointment.

**What if my child is behind on the immunisation schedule?**
Catch-up vaccinations can be planned in a longer appointment. Your GP will develop a schedule to bring your child up to date.

**Can I get vaccines outside the immunisation schedule?**
Yes. Adults, adolescents and children can be vaccinated for indications outside the NIP schedule where clinically appropriate. Your GP will advise.
