## About the specialty

Endocrinology is the medical specialty focused on hormones and the glands that produce them. Endocrinologists assess and manage conditions of the thyroid, adrenal glands, pituitary, pancreas (including diabetes), reproductive hormones, and bone metabolism. Weight management and metabolic disorders also fall within endocrinology.

Endocrine conditions often affect multiple body systems and interact with other health issues, so care is typically coordinated between the endocrinologist and your GP. Your GP identifies the concern, prepares a referral, and continues general care while the endocrinologist advises on the specific endocrine question.

## What endocrinology consultations cover

Dr Htet's clinical interests include:

- Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)

- Bone disorders including osteoporosis
- Reproductive endocrinology, including menopause and fertility-related hormonal concerns
- Gestational diabetes management and follow-up

Dr Htet holds academic positions as Clinical Lecturer in Medicine at Macquarie University and Conjoint Associate Lecturer at UNSW Medicine, alongside her role as Staff Specialist in Endocrinology at St George Hospital.

For Aurora patients, Dr Htet is Aurora's extended team endocrinologist, providing referral pathways for PCOS, gestational diabetes, menopause-related metabolic concerns and weight management in a women's health context.

## What happens at the consultation

Bring your GP referral, recent blood tests (thyroid function, HbA1c, glucose, hormone levels where relevant), imaging results where relevant, a list of your current medications, and any hospital or previous specialist letters.

The consultation involves:

- A detailed history including symptoms, family history and lifestyle factors
- A clinical examination
- Review of any tests you've already had
- A management plan that may include further investigation, medication adjustment, lifestyle recommendations, and follow-up

Some patients need short-term specialist input to establish a plan; others are followed regularly over time.

## FAQ

**Do I need to see a GP first?**
Yes. A GP referral is required for the Medicare rebate to apply.

**What should I bring?**
Your GP referral, recent blood tests and imaging results, medication list, and any hospital or previous specialist letters relevant to the reason for referral.

**Will I need tests?**
Often. Endocrinology relies heavily on blood tests, and additional imaging or dynamic testing may be arranged after the initial consultation.

**Can Dr Htet see me for weight management?**
Yes. Dr Htet is a SCOPE Certified Weight Management Specialist and manages weight and metabolic health as a substantial part of her practice.

**Can Dr Htet see me for PCOS?**
Yes. PCOS is one of Dr Htet's clinical interests, including fertility-related and metabolic aspects.

**Can Dr Htet see me for menopause-related concerns?**
Dr Htet takes referrals from Aurora GPs where menopause management involves complex hormonal, metabolic or bone health considerations. Your GP will discuss whether specialist referral is appropriate for you.
